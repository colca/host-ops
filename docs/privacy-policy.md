# COYU Host Ops SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

COYU uses Host Ops to send operational scheduling messages to cleaners who have
verbally agreed to receive them. These messages may include confirmed cleaning
dates, upcoming-stay reminders, and requests to confirm availability.

## Information we collect

We collect the cleaner's name, mobile phone number, consent status, and message
delivery records needed to operate the scheduling service.

## How we use information

We use this information only to provide and support cleaning-schedule messages,
maintain delivery records, respond to support requests, and meet legal or carrier
requirements.

## Mobile information sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in information
with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile
information may be shared only with service providers that help deliver the
messages, such as telecommunications providers, or when required by law.

## Message terms

Message frequency varies based on confirmed reservations. Message and data rates
may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out or **HELP** for assistance.

## Data retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for scheduling,
recordkeeping, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. We use reasonable
administrative and technical safeguards, but no storage or transmission method
is completely secure.
