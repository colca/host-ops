# COYU Host Ops SMS Terms and Conditions

Last updated: September 10, 2026

## Program description

COYU uses Host Ops to send operational SMS messages to cleaners about confirmed cleaning
dates, upcoming stays, and requests to confirm availability. The program does
not send marketing messages.

## Consent

Cleaners enroll only after receiving a verbal explanation of the COYU program and
explicitly agreeing to receive its messages. Consent to receive SMS messages is
not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

## Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with the reservation schedule. Messages are generally
sent 14 days and five days before a guest's check-in and one day before the related checkout
cleaning. Message and data rates may apply.

## Opt-out and help

Reply **STOP** to any message to stop receiving messages. A confirmation will be
sent after an opt-out request, and no further program messages will be sent
unless the recipient enrolls again. Reply **HELP** for assistance or email
[colca7@gmail.com](mailto:colca7@gmail.com).

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Privacy

Personal information is handled according to the
[COYU Host Ops SMS Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md). Mobile phone numbers and SMS
opt-in information are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing
or promotional purposes.

## Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time. The current version will be posted
at the same public location.
